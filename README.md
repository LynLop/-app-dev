## Bayaniverse: A Summary of the Historical Trilogy
The Bayaniverse (a portmanteau of bayani, the Filipino word for hero, and "universe") is a cinematic trilogy of historical films directed by Jerrold Tarog that offers a radical, humanized perspective on key figures in Philippine history, particularly during the Philippine Revolution and the early years of the Commonwealth. The overarching theme of the series is the deconstruction of the traditional Filipino hero—presenting them not as flawless, marble-statue figures, but as complicated, contradictory, and deeply flawed individuals wrestling with ego, betrayal, and political compromise.

1. Heneral Luna (2015): This film focuses on General Antonio Luna during the Philippine-American War. It portrays him as a brilliant but volatile and uncompromising general whose fiery temperament and brutal integrity ultimately lead to his assassination, suggesting his downfall was tied to the internal divisions and self-interest within the revolutionary government.

2. Goyo: Ang Batang Heneral (2018): Centering on the young General Gregorio del Pilar (Goyo), the film explores the theme of blind loyalty and vanity. Goyo, often seen as a romantic hero, is shown as a conflicted figure whose devotion to President Emilio Aguinaldo and his own youthful arrogance lead to his tragic end at the Battle of Tirad Pass.

3. Quezon (2025): The final installment is a biopic on Manuel L. Quezon, the first president of the Philippine Commonwealth. This film delves into the complexities of statesmanship, depicting Quezon as a charismatic, egotistical, and politically cunning leader who was a master of political maneuvering ("charm and favoritism") in his quest for Philippine independence from the United States.
